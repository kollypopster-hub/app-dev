
# Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill

## About the Anime

*Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill* is a Japanese fantasy and adventure anime series about a man named Tsuyoshi Mukouda, who gets transported to another world with a unique skill called "Online Supermarket."

Instead of becoming a powerful hero, he uses his skill to buy ingredients and cook delicious meals from a modern supermarket. Along his journey, he meets interesting companions and discovers that his cooking has extraordinary effects.

## Main Character

### Tsuyoshi Mukouda

- **Role:** Main protagonist
- **Special Skill:** Online Supermarket
- **Personality:** Kind, friendly, and practical
- **Hobby:** Cooking delicious food
